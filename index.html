About me

A bit about me

I've spent the last four years at Softzino Technologies working on software that shops and clinics use every day: the Hishabi POS, the JibonSheba diagnostic system, and more recently the single login that connects all our products.

Most of my work is on the backend with Laravel, PostgreSQL and Redis. I've made checkout stop overselling stock, made slow reports fast and made big exports stop crashing. I like owning a feature from start to finish, though, so I build the screens too, in Vue and, on the platform team, React.

I also help 3–4 junior developers on my team by reviewing their code and sitting with them when they're stuck. Outside work, I'm doing my master's at BRAC University and slowly building ProfitHishab, a tool for Facebook sellers.
